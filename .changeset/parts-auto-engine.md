New `@platformcosmo/parts-auto`: the 12 `auto:` engine part shapes (block-i4, head, valve-cover, crankshaft, camshaft, piston, rod, valve, oil-pan, intake, exhaust, timing-belt) in true millimetres, with bounded params (`cylinders` 2..8, `bore`, `valves` per cylinder), a solid-core `depthBox` that excludes protrusions, and `readParams` with did-you-mean.
