## Round 1 (first skill text)

The same four cases. Acceptance was met on the revised skill only: round 1 found the gaps below. Findings: the engine
needed two build rounds (a label clipped at the top edge; the crank drawn inside the oil pan after a too-long drop); the GPU
card image was **not rendered** (a plain dark slab in every pose); the boards built and matched the plan but `--suggest`
gave a barely visible "Covers" lift, sideways "Other parts" and a `focus` that cropped the board (the agent removed it),
and a requested `labels: "always"` label (USB, no-scale plan) was silently dropped.

## Skill gaps found and fixed

From round 1 (fixed in the references, then checked in round 2):

- hidden internals are expected in the assembled pose; keep paired parts (piston and rod) as one sub-assembly;
- a carried part moves before its carrier (or with it), against `assembly/transit-collision`;
- leave a clear gap between stages' end positions (a dropped crankshaft ended inside the oil pan);
- `focus` must keep the whole subject and labels in frame; drop or widen a cropping `focus`;
- whether `to` counts `with: "previous"` stages (it does); how to use `--suggest` when the user dictates the order;
- labelling repeated parts, crowded labels beyond `fit: 14`, keeping labels in exploded poses when a stage hides them;
- frame (`y = 0` is the back, front `+y`), default sizes of the `hw:` and `auto:` shapes; plan type mapping (power
  connector, bridge, BIOS, ...); `--suggest` prints nothing while the spec has errors;
- the image must be placed next to the spec by the user (no copying or converting by the agent);
- the board `--suggest` heuristics were synced to #103 (Components stage, mounting depth, effective placement).

From round 2 (this round): which face of `hw:gpu-card` is the front (`left`, +y); the inner dimensions of `hw:chassis-2u`
(1.5 mm walls, interior from `z = 1.5`, `y = 1.5`); merging `--suggest` JSON with `Edit`/`Write` (an agent reached for `jq` and
host python); a `tone` per kind; a caption step shifts `--png-step` numbering; in a not-to-scale plan the nominal size drives
the explode distance; grids (one counted part per row, or a counted row sub-assembly); `--packs-dir` only when
`CLAUDE_PLUGIN_DATA` is set.

## Open (engine/suggest, tracked separately)

- leader and label anchors do not follow moved parts (leaders end in empty space; piston and crank labels far from the part);
- no "Chassis tray" label on the GPU server;
- a lid lifted `+z` hides the exploded stack and its labels;
- `--suggest` emits useless nested programs for piston+rod pairs (one stage, two leaves);
- `auto:` engine shapes interpenetrate at rest at their default sizes (rods into the crank), the crank is mostly hidden
  and manifolds barely clear the block;
- same-tone parts render as one grey.

## Limits

- Same model family writes the skill, the inputs and runs the test, so results are likely optimistic. The inputs are clean
  synthetic images; real scans and photographs are untested.
- Exact plan scores come from grid-aligned inputs (see the scoring note).
- Build programs, close-ups and storyboards are not drawn in this release and are outside this test.
- Round 1 and 2 PNGs were reviewed by the orchestrator; agents viewed their own renders with `Read`.
