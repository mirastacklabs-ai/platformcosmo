Bundled fonts (D51, MiraStack Labs brand look): Latin subsets of two open-source families, both
SIL Open Font License 1.1. Upstream outlines are unmodified apart from subsetting; the family and
style names are unchanged. The license texts ship in the package at dist/LICENSES/ and in LICENSES/
of the repository (the upstream OFL.txt files, unmodified).

Both families ship static TrueType files upstream, so no variable-font instancing was needed.

Subsetting: scripts/subset-fonts.sh (fonttools 4.55.3 `pyftsubset`, installed with
`pip --require-hashes` from scripts/fonts-requirements.txt, in the container image
python@sha256:5e2bdb1b0d542d5039cffb8181c65930093949194a65c365dfb1b0fe13a4fa5b (python:3.12-slim)) keeps U+0020-007E, U+00A0-00AC, U+00AE-00FF, U+2013-2014, U+2018-2019, U+201C-201D,
U+2022, U+2026, U+20AC, U+2190-2193, U+2212, U+2248, U+2260, U+2264-2265 (Oswald has no U+2190-2193
arrows; JetBrains Mono has all), drops hinting and layout tables (no ligatures or kerning; the
engine measures plain advances) and keeps the name table. The base64 copy in
src/text/font-data.ts is generated from these files with scripts/gen-font-data.sh.
