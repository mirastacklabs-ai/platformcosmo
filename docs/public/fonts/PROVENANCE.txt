Web fonts for the docs site (platformcosmo.io), self-hosted: no Google Fonts or other third-party font
CDN is used at runtime. Both families are SIL Open Font License 1.1; the upstream license texts are
alongside (Oswald-OFL-1.1.txt, JetBrainsMono-OFL-1.1.txt, unmodified copies of LICENSES/). Upstream outlines
are unmodified apart from subsetting and WOFF2 compression; family and style names are unchanged.

The sources are the SAME upstream files as the diagram engine's fonts, with the same upstream hashes (see
packages/core/assets/fonts/PROVENANCE.txt):
